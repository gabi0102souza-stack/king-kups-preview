# Creative direction

## Thesis

A bold neighborhood food-truck page: large real food photography, black condensed type, clean light surfaces, turquoise from the truck, and small gold accents from the observed artwork.

The first screen introduces Cali-Mex in McKinney and gets visitors to the food or a catering inquiry. The food photograph occupies almost half the desktop screen, while the headline has enough weight to stand beside it. Mobile becomes a deliberate vertical composition with useful navigation and a complete food crop.

## Brand evidence and interpretation

Observed: turquoise truck; black and gold KK artwork; illustrated truck panels; bright, generous food presentation. Proposed: typographic KING KUPS wordmark, Anton display face, DM Sans body face, offset labels, thin rules and a checkered accent inspired by the photographed basket liner. The wordmark/favicon are concept treatments, not claimed official logo files. No fabricated characters, stock tacos or AI food photography.

## Rhythm

Hero food → short signature list → readable grouped menu → truck/family story → large turquoise catering section → address/hours and social updates → direct contact footer. Open editorial rows replace repeated boxed cards. Food remains visually dominant without repeating the same photo everywhere.

## Copy and accessibility

US English, short specific descriptions, no em dashes. Actual availability and prices are uncertain, so the copy makes the next contact step useful. High contrast, clear focus rings, comfortable tap targets, reduced-motion support and useful native HTML without JavaScript guide the design.
