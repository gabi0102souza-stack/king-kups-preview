# King Kups research

Research date: October 7, 2026. Business timezone: America/Chicago. This is an independent proposal, not an owner-approved website.

## Stage zero decision: proceed

The business is King Kups Official, a Cali-Mex food truck in McKinney, Texas. The official Facebook page and Instagram account were inspected directly in a browser, without signing in. They match the supplied business identity. An independent complete active website was **not found**.

An old domain **does exist**: `kingkups.com`, linked from the official Instagram bio, Roadtrippers, and an Independence Title food-truck PDF. This changes the preliminary assumption that there was no domain. DNS resolves to 74.208.236.251. Direct HTTP returned **404**, with title “Error 404 - Not found.” HTTPS for apex and www failed during TLS establishment, including an attempted diagnostic request with certificate validation skipped. No complete institutional website was served. This is evidence of an inaccessible old site, not proof the business never had a website. Domain ownership and recovery should be discussed with the owner before any official launch.

## Known hours, conflicts and uncertainty

- Official Instagram bio literally says `Tues,Fri,Sat 11pm-8:30`. This appears to contain an AM/PM typo; it must not be silently treated as an authoritative corrected schedule.
- Restaurantji (updated September 5, 2026) lists Tuesday, Friday and Saturday 11 AM to 8:30 PM, other days closed.
- Visit McKinney lists Tuesday/Friday 11 AM to 8:30 PM; Saturday closed. Its “Today” label disagreed with its weekday table during direct inspection. Do not use its dynamic label.
- Restaurant Guru includes Thursday and Saturday. Roadtrippers rendered older Wednesday/Thursday hours despite newer search snippets showing Tuesday/Friday/Saturday.
- Official Facebook's most recent public image, [post/photo](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=1738732461594147&set=a.614191034048301), announces an immediate closure due to an outside propane-line leak and mentions returning Friday. It was shown as 15 hours old on October 7. Do not infer that repairs or reopening have occurred.
- Another official post cites rain-related closure. Catering, events, weather, sellouts and food readiness can affect service.
- Preview decision: show **listed** Tuesday/Friday/Saturday hours using Restaurantji's clearly stated times, immediately advise checking the official Facebook or calling, and avoid an “open now” indicator. No claim of current availability.

## Menu and price decision

The preview is a concise selection of documented truck favorites, not a claim to publish the complete current menu. Official social channels confirm the core food; tourism and multiple corroborating listings support the additional items. Public prices differ and some menus are undated or historical. No prices, promotions, payment claims, catering packages, guest capacity or delivery availability are published. Exact recipes, allergens, current menu, prices and stock require owner confirmation.

Some directories are contaminated: MenuPix lists a Virginia Street address and Indian dishes; its unrelated cafe review further reduces trust. A generic Mexican-directory page claims breakfast/delivery and conflicting prices. These details are excluded. The New Jersey King Kups Cafe is a different business.

## Inferences

- A useful website can reduce uncertainty between discovering the food and reaching the truck, while making catering inquiries more visible.
- A phone CTA is safer than sending visitors to an inactive marketplace.
- The turquoise truck, black/gold brand artwork and colorful food support the proposed visual direction. The website typography and composition are a new concept, not an existing brand guideline.

## Access limitations and owner confirmation

Web-search fetches of Facebook/Instagram failed, but both official profiles were successfully inspected through the browser. Some news/press pages restrict automated access. Texas Monthly recognition is relayed by tourism/directories but the original award/article was not verified; no award badge is published. No new interview, live Google Business read, transaction or contact with the business occurred.

Owner confirmation needed: active location and complete weekly schedule; old domain ownership/recovery; exact menu, prices, recipes, allergens and availability; ordering process; catering service area, formats, terms and capacity; current branding and photo permissions; family-history wording. No ongoing synchronization with social platforms is implied.
