# Site strategy

## Commercial objective

Make King Kups immediately recognizable as a McKinney Cali-Mex food truck and turn interest into visits, direct contact or qualified catering inquiries. The site is a public independent proposal with no implied client approval.

## Visitor questions and answers

| Question | Response |
|---|---|
| What is King Kups? | Hero identifies Cali-Mex street food, McKinney and food-truck operation. |
| What should I try? | Signature items and a grouped selection of verified menu favorites. |
| Why make the trip? | Distinctive birria wontons, real food photos and California influence. |
| Where is it? | Address, Exxon landmark, accurate Google Maps directions link. |
| Is the truck there today? | Listed hours plus prominent official updates and phone confirmation. |
| How do I order? | Contact the truck about today's menu and order options. No unsupported online checkout. |
| Can I book catering? | A prominent section and an email-draft helper collecting date, location and guest count. |

## Content boundaries

No fabricated prices, testimonials, awards, packages, opening status or delivery link. Menu is labeled as a selection. Catering occasions are inquiry suggestions, not a claim of previously served events or guaranteed capabilities. Phone/email links work without JavaScript. The optional inquiry helper opens the visitor's email app and explicitly states it does not send or confirm a booking.

## Conversion hierarchy

Primary: menu / find truck / call. Secondary but prominent: catering inquiry. Social links supply operational updates. Persistent mobile navigation uses Find the truck and Call the truck, with catering in the main navigation and a large dedicated section.

## Launch path

Static HTML/CSS/JS, local optimized WebP photographs, local licensed fonts, relative asset paths and GitHub Pages from main. Preview carries noindex/nofollow, robots disallow and a footer concept disclosure. An official launch requires owner fact and rights approval, domain decision, updated hours/menu and removal of preview indexing restrictions.
